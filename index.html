Music Board

The top 100 songs right now on Spotify (global daily streams), Billboard Hot 100, Apple iTunes and YouTube music videos, plus an All ranking that combines the four with equal weight. Pick Current for the top 100 right now, or 1, 2, 3, 6 or 12 months to see only the songs from it released in that period (each keeps its top 100 rank). Every song links to Spotify, Apple Music, its music video and Billboard where it charts.

Data: Spotify daily chart via kworb.net, Billboard Hot 100 via a community JSON on raw.githubusercontent.com, Apple’s iTunes RSS and search API, and the YouTube Data API (US music category). All gives each chart equal weight: a song earns 101 minus its rank on each chart (0 if absent), averaged over the charts. Songs are matched across charts by title and artist, so an odd spelling can split one song into two rows. Dates are Apple release dates (YouTube upload date if Apple has none). Arrows compare each song’s rank with the same chart a week ago (NEW = not on it then); they appear once a week of history exists, except Billboard, which supplies last week’s rank. Greyed link buttons are searches because no direct link was found. Spotify, Billboard, Apple and YouTube are not affiliated with this site.
